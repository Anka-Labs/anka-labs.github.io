STEP & IGES, straight into Unity.

Drop a CAD file into the Project window and get a Unity model with the assembly hierarchy, CAD colors and pipeline-native materials. Nothing else to install.

See it in action

A house designed in Creo, exported as STEP and imported into Unity – then its materials are set up and the assembly is animated part by part.

What it does

Tessera turns exact CAD geometry into meshes you control, and keeps everything that makes the model useful: structure, names and appearance.

Keeps your CAD structure

The full assembly hierarchy with part and instance names. Repeated parts share one mesh, and mirrored instances are handled correctly.

Materials for every pipeline

Materials from CAD colors and appearances for Built-in, URP and HDRP. Names like “Steel – Satin” become material names, and metals become metallic.

A shared material library

Set up “Steel” once with your own textures and settings, and every imported model with that material uses it.

Mesh quality you control

Presets from Draft to Ultra, or exact chordal error and angular tolerance. Welding closes seams but keeps sharp CAD edges.

Three ways in

Drag and drop like an FBX, a window for files outside the project, and a scripting API for batch conversion and CI.

Runtime import on Windows

CadLoader loads STEP and IGES files while your app runs, in play mode and in Windows x64 builds.

What you can build

Getting CAD data into a real-time engine used to mean converting files through other software, losing the assembly structure and fixing materials by hand. With Tessera, a STEP file goes from your CAD tool to Unity in seconds, with every part named and in place.

Product presentations

Show real products in their actual design, interactive and in real time, in configurators and sales tools.

Digital twins & simulation

Bring machines and production lines into Unity with the full assembly, ready to animate and connect to live data.

Training, VR & AR

Build assembly and maintenance instructions from the same model the engineers work in.

Architecture & construction

Walk through a building, or show how it is put together, step by step.

Design reviews

Check fit and appearance in context, with your own lighting and materials.

Your users' own files

With runtime import, your Windows application can open the STEP files your users bring.

Because the hierarchy and part names come straight from CAD, you can script against them – for example to animate an assembly part by part, in the order it is built.

How it works

A bundled converter built on Open CASCADE reads and tessellates the CAD file in the background, so the editor stays responsive even on large assemblies.

CAD file

STEP AP203, AP214, AP242 or IGES, from any CAD tool.

→

Tessellation

Open CASCADE reads the B-rep geometry, repairs broken faces and builds the meshes.

→

Unity model

Hierarchy, meshes with normals, tangents and UVs, and materials for your pipeline.
